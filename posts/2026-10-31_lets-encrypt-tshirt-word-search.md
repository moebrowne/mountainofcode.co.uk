# 🔒 Let's Encrypt T-Shirt Word Search

#Lets Encrypt


I have been using certificates from [Let's Encrypt](https://letsencrypt.org/) for over ten years, [since the beta](/lets-encrypt-certificate-issued)
in fact so when I saw they were making [t-shirts](https://letsencrypt.org/2026/08/11/root-shirt) with the [X1 root cert](https://letsencrypt.org/certs/isrgrootx1.pem)
printed on, I just had to have one. It is _the_ geekest t-shirt I own, and that is saying something!

After wearing it for a while, I started to notice small words in the base64 string. Don't get me wrong, I don't think
this is some kind of conspiracy, I'm sure almost all long base64 strings have this.

It became a word search:
