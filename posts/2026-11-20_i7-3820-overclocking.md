# Overclocking An Intel i7-3820 🔥

#CPU
#overclocking

I'd always assumed that overclocking a CPU required a special model of CPU/motherboard. That mindset was broken when I
came across [this post](https://www.reddit.com/r/buildapc/comments/18fjx4/guide_so_youre_trying_to_overclock_the_i73820_i/).
It gives straight forward instructions for overclocking a 3.6 GHz Intel i7-3820, the exact CPU I happened to have.

I don't claim to understand exactly how it works, but my understanding is that the motherboard sets a reference
frequency which everything else (CPU, RAM, PCIe, etc) syncs to. Increasing the 'base' = speed everything up.

This caught me out at first. I didn't understand why the article was messing with the RAM speed/timings and assumed they
were also overclocking that too, so left mine as they were, assuming they were unchanged. This caused a number of random
crashes until I realised and set the correct RAM speed.

The settings I ended up changing were:

- CPU Base: auto > manual
- Processor base clock: 1.00x > 1.25x
- CPU Clock Ratio: 36 > 37
- Turbo Boost Tech: enabled > disabled
- PWM phase control: auto > high performance
- CPU load line calibration: auto > extreme
- CPU Vcore: 1.245v > 1.320v


This is enabled a CPU frequency of 4.6 GHz which is about +30% extra performance.
